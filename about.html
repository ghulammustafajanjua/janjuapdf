About JanjuaPDF

JanjuaPDF is a free online toolbox for everyday PDF jobs. Our goal is simple: give you fast, easy PDF tools that cost nothing, need no signup, and respect your privacy.

What you can do

JanjuaPDF has 32 free tools. Every one works in your browser, on a phone or a computer.

Organize pages

  • Merge PDF - Combine many PDFs into one file, in the order you want.
  • Split PDF - Extract pages or split every page into its own file.
  • Extract PDF Pages - Pick the pages you need and save them as a new PDF.
  • Organize PDF - See every page, then drag to reorder, rotate or remove pages.
  • Delete PDF Pages - Remove the pages you do not need from a PDF.
  • Reorder PDF Pages - Change the order of pages or reverse the whole document.
  • Rotate PDF - Rotate all pages of a PDF.
  • Crop PDF - Cut away the edges of your PDF pages and keep only the part you need.
  • Add Page Numbers - Number the pages of your PDF.

Edit and sign

  • Edit PDF - Add text, white-out boxes and images to your PDF.
  • Sign PDF - Draw, type or upload your signature and place it on your PDF.
  • Fill PDF Form - Type into the boxes of a fillable PDF form and save it.
  • Create Fillable PDF - Add text boxes, checkboxes and dropdowns to turn a PDF into a form.
  • Add Watermark - Stamp text across every page.
  • Redact PDF - Black out names, numbers and other private details for good.

Compress, protect, repair and compare

  • Compress PDF - Make a PDF smaller so it is easy to email or upload.
  • Protect PDF - Add a password so only people who know it can open your PDF.
  • Unlock PDF - Remove the password from your own PDF when you know it.
  • Repair PDF - Fix a damaged PDF that will not open properly.
  • Compare PDF - See what changed between two versions of a PDF.

Convert to PDF

  • Images to PDF - Turn JPG or PNG images into a single PDF.
  • Scan to PDF - Use your phone camera to scan documents into a clean PDF.
  • Word to PDF - Turn a Word document (.docx) into a PDF.
  • Excel to PDF - Turn Excel sheets (.xlsx) or CSV files into a PDF.

Convert from PDF

  • PDF to JPG - Convert every PDF page into a JPG image.
  • PDF to PNG - Convert every PDF page into a sharp PNG image.
  • PDF to Word - Copy the text of your PDF into an editable Word file.
  • PDF to Excel - Copy tables from a PDF into an Excel spreadsheet.
  • PDF to PowerPoint - Turn every PDF page into a PowerPoint slide.
  • PDF to Text - Extract the text from a PDF and save it as a TXT file.
  • PDF to Markdown - Turn the text of your PDF into clean Markdown.
  • OCR PDF - Make a scanned PDF searchable and copy its text.

Your files stay with you

Our tools run directly inside your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to our servers. When you close the page, your files are gone from the tool.
