About JanjuaPDF

JanjuaPDF is a free online toolbox for everyday PDF jobs. Our goal is simple: give you fast, easy PDF tools that cost nothing, need no signup, and respect your privacy.

What you can do

  • Merge PDF - combine several PDF files into one document.
  • Compress PDF - make a PDF smaller for email and uploads.
  • Split PDF - separate every page, or extract the page ranges you choose.
  • Rotate PDF - turn pages by 90, 180 or 270 degrees.
  • Images to PDF - turn JPG and PNG pictures into a PDF.
  • PDF to JPG and PDF to PNG - save each PDF page as an image.
  • Add watermark - place your own text across every page.
  • Add page numbers - number the pages of a PDF.
  • Delete and reorder pages - remove pages or change their order.
  • PDF to Text - extract the text of a PDF into a TXT file.

Your files stay with you

Our tools run directly inside your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to our servers. When you close the page, your files are gone from the tool.
