    How to make a PDF fillable

    1. Choose your PDF, for example an application or order form you made in Word.
    2. Press Text box, Big text box, Checkbox or Dropdown. The field appears on the page.
    3. Drag the field onto the empty line or box, and drag the small red square to change its size.
    4. Click a field to give it a clear name, such as Full name. For a dropdown, type the choices separated by commas.
    5. Repeat for every field, on every page, then press Create Fillable PDF and download.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is it free to create a fillable PDF?

    Yes. It is free, needs no account and adds no watermark.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. The form fields are added inside your browser, so your file stays on your device.

    Where can people fill in my form?

    In almost any PDF reader, such as Adobe Acrobat Reader, Chrome, Edge or Firefox, and on phones. You can test it right away with our free Fill PDF Form tool.

    What is the difference between a text box and a big text box?

    A text box holds one line, such as a name or phone number. A big text box allows several lines, for comments or an address.

    My PDF already has some fields. Will they be lost?

    No. Existing fields are kept and your new fields are added. Each field just needs its own name.

    Can I add a signature field?

    You can add a text box for a typed name. To sign a PDF with a drawn signature, use Sign PDF.
