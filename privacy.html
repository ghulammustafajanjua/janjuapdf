Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how JanjuaPDF (https://janjuapdf.com) handles information when you use our website.

Your files

All PDF and image processing happens inside your own web browser. Your files are not uploaded to, stored on, or seen by our servers.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or files. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may automatically record basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and to keep the site running.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site, for example which pages and tools are most popular and how people find us. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as the pages you visit, approximate location, device and browser type. It does not receive your PDF or image files. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services and opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Third-party libraries

To work, our tools load open-source code libraries (such as pdf-lib, PDF.js and JSZip) from public content networks (cdnjs, run by Cloudflare, and jsDelivr). Your browser may share basic technical data, such as your IP address, with that network when loading them.

Children

Our site is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
