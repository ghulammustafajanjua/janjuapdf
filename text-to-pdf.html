How to use Text to PDF

  1. Type or paste your text in the box, or tap "open a .txt file" to load a text file from your phone or computer.
  2. Choose the page size, the text size and a file name.
  3. Press Create PDF and download your file. Long text continues onto as many pages as needed.

When to use Text to PDF

Sometimes you only have plain text: notes copied from a website, a message you wrote on your phone, a list, or a .txt file from an old computer. Many forms, offices and teachers want a PDF instead. This tool turns the text into a neat document with normal margins and page breaks, ready to print, email or upload.

Empty lines in your text are kept as gaps between paragraphs, and long lines wrap to the next line automatically.

Good to know

  • English text is saved as real text, so it can be selected and searched in the PDF.
  • Urdu, Arabic, Hindi and other scripts are drawn as images so the letters join and read correctly from right to left where needed. In those files the text cannot be selected.
  • Need headings, bold text or tables? Write the document in Word and use Word to PDF instead.
  • Want to add the new PDF to other files? Use Combine Files to PDF or Merge PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Can I write in Urdu?

Yes. Type or paste Urdu text and press Create PDF. Lines in Urdu or Arabic are written from right to left, as they should be.

Can I convert a .txt file to PDF?

Yes. Tap "open a .txt file", choose the file, check the text in the box and press Create PDF.

Is there a length limit?

There is no fixed limit, and the PDF gets as many pages as needed. Very long text, such as a whole book, can take a little longer on an older phone.

Can I change the font?

The tool uses a clean standard font so the file looks the same on every device. You can choose the text size and the page size.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The PDF is created in your browser on your own device, so your text never leaves your phone or computer.
