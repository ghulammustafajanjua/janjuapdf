    How to extract pages from a PDF

    1. Choose your PDF file. Small previews of every page appear.
    2. Tap the pages you want, or type them, for example 1-3, 5, 8.
    3. To get every page as its own file, tick Save each selected page as its own PDF.
    4. Press Extract PDF Pages and download.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is it free to extract pages?

    Yes. It is free, needs no account and adds no watermark.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. Pages are copied in your browser, so your file stays on your device.

    Will the extracted pages lose quality?

    No. Pages are copied exactly, with the same text, images and links.

    What is the difference from Split PDF?

    Extract keeps only the pages you choose. Split PDF can also cut a whole document into one file per page.

    Can I remove pages instead?

    Yes. To delete a few pages and keep the rest, use Delete PDF Pages.
