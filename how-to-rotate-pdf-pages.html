How to Rotate a PDF and Save It Facing the Right Way

Published October 13, 2026 · Updated October 24, 2026 · JanjuaPDF guides

Rotate a PDF now

Scanned papers and phone scans often end up sideways or upside down. Turning the page on your screen helps only until you close the file. To fix it for good, you need to rotate the PDF and save the new version. Here is how to do it in a minute.

Step by step: rotate every page

  1. Open the Rotate PDF tool.
  2. Choose your PDF file.
  3. Select how far to turn it: 90 degrees clockwise, 180 degrees, or 90 degrees counter-clockwise.
  4. Press Run and download the rotated PDF.
  5. Open it once to check that the pages face the right way.

Which option should I choose?

What you seeChoose

A quick way to decide: look at where the first line of text is. Turn the page so that line moves to the top. If you pick the wrong direction, simply run the tool again on the new file.

Only one or two pages are sideways?

This is very common with scanned documents, for example when one page of a form was scanned the other way round. The Rotate PDF tool turns all pages, so for single pages use Organize PDF instead:

  1. Open Organize PDF and choose your file. A small preview of every page appears.
  2. Find the sideways page and tap the ↻ button on it until it faces the right way. Each tap turns it 90 degrees.
  3. Repeat for any other wrong pages, then save the new PDF.

Because you can see every page, this is also the safest way to check a long scan before you send it.

Why does my PDF open sideways again?

Most PDF readers, including the ones in phones and web browsers, have a rotate button that only changes the view. The file itself stays the same, so the next person who opens it, or you on another device, sees it sideways again. To fix the problem for everyone, the rotation has to be saved into the file, which is exactly what the tools above do.

Portrait and landscape pages

Turning a page by 90 degrees also changes its shape. A tall (portrait) page becomes a wide (landscape) page. This is correct for tables, charts and spreadsheets that were printed sideways. When you print a mixed document, most printers handle each page's orientation automatically, but it is worth checking the print preview first.

Tips for scans made with a phone

  • Hold the phone straight above the paper, with the top of the page at the top of the screen. Most sideways scans come from tilting the phone.
  • Scan all pages the same way round. Then, if they are wrong, one rotation fixes them all.
  • After rotating, compress the PDF if it is too big to email or upload.

Frequently asked questions

Does rotating a PDF reduce its quality?

No. Only the rotation setting of the page changes. Text and images are not recompressed, so the file looks exactly the same, just turned.

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Use Organize PDF, tap the rotate button on that page only, and save. The Rotate PDF tool is for turning all pages at once.

Can I rotate a password-protected PDF?

First remove the password with Unlock PDF. You need to know the password; the tool does not crack passwords. Then rotate the unlocked copy.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Both tools work in the browser on Android phones and iPhones, with no app to install.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The PDF is processed inside your own browser and is not sent to our servers.
