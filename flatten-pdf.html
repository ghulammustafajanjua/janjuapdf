    How to use Flatten PDF

    1. Choose your filled PDF form.
    2. Pick a method. Form fields locks the boxes, checkboxes and dropdowns and keeps the page sharp. Everything also locks comments, stamps and drawings, but turns each page into a picture.
    3. Press Flatten PDF and download the locked file. Open it once to check that all your answers are still there.

    When to use Flatten PDF

    A fillable PDF form keeps its answers in boxes that anyone can click and change. Before you send a completed application, contract or invoice, flattening turns those answers into normal page content. The person who receives the file sees exactly what you filled in, and the answers cannot be edited by accident.

    Flattening also helps when a form looks different on another phone or computer. Some PDF viewers do not show the values inside form fields, but a flattened file looks the same everywhere and prints correctly.

    Good to know

    • Flattening cannot be undone, so keep a copy of the original file in case you need to change an answer later.
    • Need to fill in the form first? Use Fill PDF Form, then flatten the saved file here.
    • If the Form fields method shows an error, for example because the answers use Urdu, Arabic or other special letters, choose Everything instead.
    • Everything makes the text unselectable. If you need searchable text afterwards, use OCR PDF.

    Frequently asked questions

    What does it mean to flatten a PDF?

    Flattening merges the interactive parts of a PDF, such as form fields, into the page itself. The text and ticks stay visible, but they are no longer boxes that can be clicked or edited.

    Which method should I choose?

    Choose Form fields for normal fillable forms. The page stays sharp and the text can still be selected. Choose Everything if the file also has comments, stamps or drawings that you want to lock, or if the first method shows an error.

    Can someone still edit a flattened PDF?

    The answers can no longer be changed in form boxes. A person with a PDF editor can still add new text on top, as with any PDF, so flattening protects against accidental changes, not against forgery.

    What if my PDF has no form fields?

    Then it is already flat, and the tool will tell you. If it still has comments or stamps you want to lock, use the Everything method.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. Flattening happens in your browser on your own device, so the file never leaves your phone or computer.
