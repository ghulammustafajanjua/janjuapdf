How to use CV Maker

  1. Fill in your name, job title and contact details. You can add a photo if the job asks for one.
  2. Add your jobs and your education. For each job, write your duties and achievements one per line.
  3. Add your skills and languages, choose a colour, then press Create CV PDF and download your CV.

When to use CV Maker

Most jobs ask for a CV as a PDF. A PDF looks the same on every phone and computer, so the employer sees your CV exactly as you made it. This tool gives you a clean one-column layout with clear headings, which is easy for recruiters to read and for job websites to scan.

You do not need Word or a design app. If you tick "Remember my CV", you can come back later on the same phone or computer, update your latest job and download a new copy in a minute.

Tips for a strong CV

  • Keep it short: one or two pages is enough for most jobs.
  • Start each bullet with an action word, such as Managed, Delivered, Trained or Increased, and add numbers where you can, for example "Managed 25 riders".
  • Use a professional email address and check your phone number twice.
  • Add a photo only if it is normal for jobs in your country or the job advert asks for one.
  • Need to send your certificates too? Put them together with your CV using Combine Files to PDF, and make the file smaller with Compress PDF if the upload limit is small.

Frequently asked questions

Is this CV maker free?

Yes. You can make and download as many CVs as you like, with no signup and no watermark.

Can I make my CV in Urdu or Arabic?

Yes. If you use Urdu, Arabic or other special letters, the CV is drawn as an image so the letters look right. In English, the text in the PDF stays real text that job websites can read.

Is a CV the same as a resume?

They are very similar. Resume is the word used mostly in the USA and Canada, and CV is used in the UK, the Gulf, Pakistan and India. This tool works for both.

Where is my CV saved?

Only in this browser on your own device, and only if "Remember my CV" is ticked. Untick it to remove the saved copy.

Is my information uploaded?

No. The PDF is created in your browser, so your personal details never leave your phone or computer.
