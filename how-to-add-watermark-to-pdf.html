How to Add a Text Watermark to a PDF (Free)

Published October 13, 2026 · Updated October 24, 2026 · JanjuaPDF guides

Add a watermark now

A watermark is a faint word or phrase that appears behind or across the content of a page, such as CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT or your own name. People use it to mark unfinished documents, show who a copy was prepared for, or discourage careless reuse of a file.

Step by step: add a watermark

  1. Open the Add Watermark tool.
  2. Choose your PDF file.
  3. Type your watermark text.
  4. Set the font size (48 is the default) and the opacity (0.25 is the default).
  5. Press Run, download the watermarked PDF and open it to check that the page is still easy to read.

What the watermark looks like

Your text is placed diagonally across the middle of every page, in a soft grey and semi-transparent, so the content underneath stays readable.

Choosing size and opacity

OpacityResultGood for
0.1 to 0.2Very light, easy to overlookBrochures and price lists where you only want a soft brand mark
0.25 to 0.35Clearly visible, text still easy to readMost documents: drafts, copies, samples
0.4 to 0.6Strong and hard to ignoreID copies and documents you want to protect from reuse
  • If a long phrase runs off the page, lower the font size or use shorter text.
  • Short words look cleanest: DRAFT, COPY, SAMPLE.

Protect copies of your ID, passport or Emirates ID

When a company, landlord or agent asks for a copy of your passport, visa, Emirates ID or another ID card, a watermark that says who the copy is for and why makes it much harder to misuse. For example:

  • For ABC Properties tenancy only - Oct 2026
  • Copy for bank account opening - XYZ Bank
  • Job application - not valid for other use

Use an opacity of about 0.4, and keep the text short enough to fit across the card. If the copy is also too big to email, compress it afterwards.

Ideas for what to write

  • DRAFT for documents that are not final.
  • CONFIDENTIAL for private papers.
  • SAMPLE for designs, CV templates or work you show to a client before payment.
  • For [name] only for a copy prepared for one person.
  • Your business name on a price list or catalogue.

Watermark, password or redaction: which do I need?

You want to...Use
Show that a document is a draft, a copy or for one purpose onlyWatermark

A watermark is a clear visual message, not a lock. Someone with editing software may still be able to remove it, so for sensitive files combine it with a password or redaction. This tool adds text watermarks; it does not place a logo or picture.

Tips

  • Keep your original PDF and save the watermarked copy under a new name, such as contract-DRAFT.pdf.
  • Add the watermark last, after merging or reordering pages.
  • You can also number the pages with Add Page Numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add a watermark to only some pages?

The tool adds it to every page. To mark only some pages, split them off with Split PDF, add the watermark, and merge the files again.

Can I add my logo as a watermark?

Not with this tool. It adds text only. You can type your business name instead.

Can a watermark be removed?

Sometimes, by someone with PDF editing software. It is a strong signal, not a security feature. For real protection, also use a password or redaction.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It runs in the browser on Android phones and iPhones, with no app to install.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The watermark is added inside your own browser, and your file is not sent to our servers.
