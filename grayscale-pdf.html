Turn a colour PDF into black and white (grayscale) for cheaper printing or forms that ask for it.

    How to use Grayscale PDF

    1. Choose your PDF file.
    2. Pick the quality. High keeps small text sharp. Standard gives a smaller file.
    3. Press Convert to Grayscale, wait while each page is changed, then download your black and white PDF.

    When to use Grayscale PDF

    Colour pages use a lot of expensive colour ink. If you only need to read a document, a grayscale copy prints with black ink only and often looks cleaner. Some offices, schools and online forms also ask for black and white scans of documents.

    The tool changes every colour on every page into a shade of gray, including photos, logos, stamps and coloured text. The page size and the order of the pages stay the same.

    Good to know

    • Each page is saved as a picture. This means text can no longer be selected or searched, and links and form fields are removed. Keep your original file if you need those.
    • To make the text searchable again, run the new file through OCR PDF.
    • A grayscale file can be bigger than the original when the original has mostly plain text. If you need a small file for an upload form, use Compress PDF with a target size afterwards.
    • Making a new scan with your phone? Scan to PDF can create a black and white scan directly.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is grayscale the same as greyscale?

    Yes. Grayscale is the American spelling and greyscale is the British spelling. Both mean a picture made only of black, white and shades of gray.

    Will my PDF still look the same?

    Yes, apart from the colours. Layout, page size and page order do not change. Coloured areas become lighter or darker gray depending on how bright the colour was.

    Can I still copy the text after converting?

    No. Every page becomes an image, so the text cannot be selected. Use OCR PDF on the new file if you need searchable text.

    Does it work with password-protected PDFs?

    If the PDF needs a password to open, remove it first with Unlock PDF (only for your own files when you know the password), then convert it.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. The conversion happens in your browser on your own device, so the file never leaves your phone or computer.
