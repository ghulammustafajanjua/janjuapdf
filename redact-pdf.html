    How to redact a PDF

    1. Choose your PDF file.
    2. Type a word or number you want to hide, such as a name, phone number or passport number, and press Find & mark. Every place it appears is marked.
    3. Drag on the page to draw extra black boxes by hand. Use the arrows to check every page, and tap × to remove a box.
    4. Press Redact PDF and download your file. The marked content is removed for good.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the difference between redacting and covering?

    Covering, for example with a white or black box in an editor, only hides the text. It can often still be copied or found. Redacting removes it. Here, every page with a black box is rebuilt as a clean image with the box burned in, so the hidden text is gone.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. Everything happens in your browser. That matters most for the private documents people usually redact.

    Will the redacted pages still have selectable text?

    No. Pages you redact become images, so their text can no longer be selected or searched. Pages without any black box stay exactly as they were.

    Why did Find not find my word?

    Scanned pages are pictures and contain no searchable text. Draw the boxes by hand on those pages.

    Should I check the result?

    Yes. Always open the redacted file and look at every page before you share it.
