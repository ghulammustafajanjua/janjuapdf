    How to crop a PDF

    1. Choose your PDF file. The first page appears with a crop box on it.
    2. Drag the corners of the box so it covers the part you want to keep. Drag the middle of the box to move it.
    3. Choose whether to crop all pages the same way or only this page. Use the arrows to check other pages.
    4. Press Crop PDF and download your file.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. Cropping is free, needs no account and adds no watermark.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. Everything happens in your browser, so your file stays on your device.

    Does cropping lower the quality?

    No. Text and images keep their quality. Only the visible area of the page changes.

    Is the cut-off part deleted?

    It is hidden, not erased. Most people never see it, but special software can still find it. To remove private information for good, use a redaction tool instead.

    Can I crop scanned pages?

    Yes. Cropping is a quick way to remove dark scanner edges or extra background from phone scans.
