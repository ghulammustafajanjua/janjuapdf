    How to use Split PDF

    1. Choose the PDF you want to split.
    2. Pick a mode: every page as a separate PDF (downloaded as a ZIP), or one PDF made from a page range such as 1-3,5.
    3. Press Run and download the result.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I extract only some pages?

    Choose the range mode and type the pages, for example 1-3,5,8-10. You will get one new PDF with just those pages.

    What does the ZIP contain?

    One PDF per page of your original document.

    Is my document uploaded?

    No. Splitting runs in your browser and your file stays on your device.
