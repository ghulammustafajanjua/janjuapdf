    How to use Merge PDF

    1. Click the box and choose two or more PDF files, or drag them in.
    2. Use the up and down arrows to put the files in the order you want.
    3. Press Run, then download your merged PDF.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is it free to merge PDF files?

    Yes. JanjuaPDF is free, with no signup and no limit that we enforce.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Merging happens inside your own browser, so your PDFs never leave your device.

    Can I change the order of the files?

    Yes. Use the arrow buttons next to each file before you press Run.
