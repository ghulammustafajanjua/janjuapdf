    How to edit a PDF

    1. Choose your PDF file. The first page appears below.
    2. Press Add text and type, for example your name, a date or an answer on a form. Change the size and colour at the top.
    3. Press White-out box to cover something with a white (or coloured) rectangle, then type new text on top if you like.
    4. Press Add image to place a logo, stamp or photo. Drag items to move them and use the corner square to resize.
    5. Use the arrows to go to other pages, then press Edit PDF and download your file.

    When to use Edit PDF

    PDFs are made to look the same everywhere, which also makes them hard to change. Often you only need a small edit: write your name and date on a form that has no boxes, add a stamp or logo, cover an old phone number and type the new one, or add a short note for the reader.

    This tool lets you place text, white-out boxes and images on any page without installing software.

    Tips for the best result

    • Zoom in on your phone or computer to place text exactly on the line.
    • Match the text size to the rest of the page so your additions look neat.
    • For forms with real fillable boxes, Fill PDF Form is quicker.
    • A white-out box only covers text. To remove private information for good, use Redact PDF.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is it free to edit a PDF here?

    Yes. Editing is free, needs no account and adds no watermark.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. Everything happens in your browser, so your document stays on your device.

    My PDF is a form with boxes. Is there an easier way?

    Yes. If the form has real fillable boxes, use Fill PDF Form to type straight into them.

    Can I change the existing text in the PDF?

    Not directly. Cover the old text with a white-out box, then add your new text on top. To the reader it looks like the text was changed.

    Does a white-out box delete the text underneath?

    No. It only covers it, and the original text can still be copied from the file. To remove private information for good, use Redact PDF.

    Can I type in Urdu, Arabic or Hindi?

    Yes. Text in other scripts is added as a sharp image of your text, so it looks right on every device.
