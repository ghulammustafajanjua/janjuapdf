How to Edit a PDF for Free (Add Text, Images and White-Out)

Published October 14, 2026 · JanjuaPDF guides

Edit a PDF now

PDFs are made to look the same everywhere, which also makes them feel hard to change. But for most everyday jobs, like adding your name, fixing a date, covering a mistake or adding a logo, you do not need expensive software. Here is how to do each one for free.

Short version: open Edit PDF, choose your file, use Add text, White-out box or Add image, drag things into place and press Edit PDF.

Add text anywhere

  1. Open Edit PDF and choose your PDF.
  2. Press Add text and type. Pick the size and colour at the top.
  3. Drag the text by its small round handle to the right spot. Use the arrows to edit other pages.
  4. Press Edit PDF and download your file.

You can also type in Urdu, Arabic or Hindi. That text is added as a sharp image so it looks right on every device.

Change existing text (white-out and retype)

You cannot click into the original words of most PDFs. Do what people do on paper: cover and rewrite.

  1. Press White-out box, drag it over the old text and resize it with the corner square.
  2. Press Add text and type the new words on top.

Important: a white-out box only covers text. The old words can still be found and copied from the file. To remove private details for good, use Redact PDF instead.

Add a logo, stamp or photo

Press Add image, choose a picture, then drag it into place and resize it from the corner. PNG images with a transparent background look best for logos and stamps.

Other edits, the easy way

  • Form with boxes? Type straight into them with Fill PDF Form.
  • Need a signature? Use Sign PDF.
  • Pages in the wrong order, sideways or extra? Fix them with Organize PDF.
  • Edges or margins to remove? Use Crop PDF.
  • Need to rewrite a lot of text? Copy it into Word with PDF to Word, edit there, then save as PDF again.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to edit a PDF?

Yes. All these tools are free, need no account and add no watermark.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Editing happens in your browser, so your file stays on your device.

Can I edit a scanned PDF?

Yes. You can add text, white-out and images on top of a scan. To copy or search its text, run it through OCR PDF first.

Can I edit a PDF on my phone?

Yes. Every tool works in your phone's browser on Android and iPhone.

Why can I not click on the original text?

Most PDFs store text as fixed positions, not as an editable document. Cover and retype small changes, or convert to Word for big rewrites.
