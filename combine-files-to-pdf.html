    How to use Combine Files to PDF

    1. Choose your files. You can pick photos (JPG, PNG or WebP) and PDF files at the same time, and add more files later.
    2. Use the arrows to put the files in the right order, and × to remove a file you do not need.
    3. Pick the page size for photos, press Combine to PDF and download your single PDF.

    When to use Combine Files to PDF

    Many online forms for jobs, visas, admissions and bank accounts allow only one upload. Your documents, however, are often mixed: a CV saved as a PDF, a photo of your ID card, a picture of a certificate and a scanned letter. This tool puts all of them into one PDF so you can upload or email a single file.

    Every page of each PDF is kept as it is, and every photo becomes one page. The final file follows the order you set in the list.

    Tips for the best result

    • Choose A4 when the file will be printed or sent to an office. Each photo is placed in the middle of an A4 page, turned to landscape when the photo is wide.
    • Take photos of papers in daylight and hold the phone straight above the page.
    • If the combined file is too big for an upload form, use Compress PDF with a target size.
    • Only PDFs? Merge PDF does the same job. Only photos? JPG to PDF has extra options.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I combine JPG and PDF files into one PDF?

    Yes. Select your photos and PDF files together, put them in order and press Combine to PDF. The result is one PDF file.

    How many files can I add?

    There is no fixed limit. Very large files or hundreds of photos can be slow on an older phone, so for big jobs a computer works best.

    Will my PDF pages lose quality?

    No. Pages from your PDF files are copied as they are, so text stays sharp and selectable. Photos are saved at good quality.

    What about password-protected PDFs?

    A PDF that needs a password to open cannot be combined. Remove the password first with Unlock PDF (only for your own files when you know the password).

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Everything happens in your browser on your own device, so your documents never leave your phone or computer.
